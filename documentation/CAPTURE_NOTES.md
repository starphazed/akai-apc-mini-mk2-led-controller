# Capture and editing notes

## What the documentation establishes

The virtual screenshots and supplied recordings show cell editing, animated backgrounds, reactive controls and matrix output. The separate hardware recording shows the physical APC Mini Mk2 lights and pad interactions. It does not show the off-camera software settings, so hardware clip titles describe visible behaviour rather than assuming exact effect names.

The network screenshot documents the inspected modules. `chase_example` was disconnected. A visible `moviefilein1` test pattern supplied the inspected media input. The later screen recording shows IMAGE / TOP selected and a multicolour matrix, without separately establishing every internal signal or resolution.

## Media treatment

- Seven original virtual screenshots were delivered on a 1920×1080 canvas. The approximately 1707×1019 window captures were centred without upscaling. Personal filesystem title bars were cropped out.
- The virtual recording was cropped from 2558×1598 to the controller or controller plus settings, then scaled proportionally and padded to 1920×1080.
- Hardware footage retained its full framing. The 1840×1034 image was slightly enlarged and minimally padded to match the 1920×1080 delivery size.
- Every clip is a continuous excerpt at its original playback speed, encoded at 30 fps. No visual effects, simulated footage, motion interpolation or colour redesign was added.
- Exports are silent. The screen recording was effectively silent; the hardware recording's audio was omitted to match. Original recordings remain unchanged.
- The hardware hero image is an actual frame from the hands-on pad response clip, not a generated image.
- All fifteen video exports passed full decoding checks and were visually sampled across their duration.

## Limitations

Some screenshots retain the capture pointer/glow. The early virtual interface screenshots display a MIDI send error; a later session showed MIDI enabled and increasing packet counts. The separate hardware footage documents visible physical behaviour, without establishing that the two recordings are synchronised.

The enlarged pixel-output viewer uses smooth filtering. Neither that image nor its viewer dimensions independently establishes an exact source TOP resolution. In the virtual Fill All clip, later colour edits are pending editor values, not evidence that another change was applied.

The hardware footage retains its camera movement, LED glow, exposure and occasional hand occlusion. Exact off-camera software controls cannot be inferred from that footage alone.

## Preservation

Original recordings and synced source files were not modified. Capturing the earlier screenshots involved temporary manual-cell edits and view changes; the session reported its original white manual board and External mode restored, with no project save. Video editing did not change the TouchDesigner project.
